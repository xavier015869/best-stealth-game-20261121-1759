**Moonvault** — a top-down stealth game. You are Wren, a thief with three nights to steal the Moonstone from a guarded vault and reach the EXIT unseen. Lantern Guards patrol learnable loops with wall-clipped vision cones; crates and statues are cover, shadow rugs make you hard to see, and a suspicion meter (suspicious → investigate → chase) always gives you a chance to recover. Three gaits — sneak, walk, sprint — trade speed for noise, and Night Three adds a sweeping brass Watch-Eye whose alarm sends every guard to your last-seen spot. All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized WebAudio: zero assets, zero network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — title → briefing → play. Move with WASD or arrows, hold C to sneak, hold Shift to sprint, R retries a night.
